# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Nightcore Browser does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or to any third party, and the extension makes no network requests.

## What the extension accesses

The extension runs on every page so that it can apply the nightcore effect to any audio or video. On each page it:

- Changes the playback speed and pitch of `<audio>` and `<video>` elements while the effect is enabled.
- Routes the audio of those elements through the browser's Web Audio API to apply the bass boost. All audio processing happens locally in your browser.

It does not read the content of the pages you visit, other than finding the audio and video elements on them.

## What the extension stores

Your settings (whether the effect is enabled, the speed and the bass boost level) are saved in your browser's local extension storage so they are restored on every page. They never leave your device.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data anywhere.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not access your browsing history, cookies, or other tabs' content.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this file, along with the date it was last updated.
